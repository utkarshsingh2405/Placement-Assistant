Questions and answers.

Can I access both the Institute and Placement systems with one account?

Yes. Your institutional email and password authenticate you across both desks. Switch between them anytime using the top navigation bar or the Alt + D keyboard shortcut.

What happens if my CGPA or backlogs change in the academic records?

The Academic Institute registrar records update directly into the Placement drive eligibility filter in real time. Whenever a grade update is approved, eligible drives immediately update on your dashboard.

How do I view my hall ticket on test day?

Your perforated hall ticket activates on your Placement dashboard as soon as you qualify for that round, including the QR code for door check-in, syllabus breakdown, and reporting gate closure time.
